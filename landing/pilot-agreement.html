Part A. What you buy

1. The back-test

Every supplier invoice line dated in [month, year] ("the test month") is checked against its purchase order line and against your receiving record, counting partial deliveries, earlier invoices and credit notes on the same purchase order whatever their date. You receive a report listing only the lines that do not agree, each with its amount and the documents behind it.

2. The pilot

For 30 days from the day your account is ready, new supplier invoices are checked the same way in the MatchDesk application, with a weekly summary by email.

3. Fee

[CAD 500] plus applicable taxes, paid before work starts, in your account's currency. Nothing else is charged during the pilot. A monthly plan starts on day 31 only if you sign a separate order form.

4. Delivery

The first draft report is delivered within 15 business days of receiving everything in clause 5. Delivery method (tick one): ☐ in the MatchDesk application ☐ prepared by us by hand from reports you export into a folder you own.

Part B. What you supply

5. Records

  1. Your purchase orders, bills and vendor credits: through a read-only QuickBooks Online connection your controller makes, or as reports you export.
  2. Supplier invoice and credit-note files (PDF or image) dated in the test month.
  3. For every purchase order those documents name: the earlier invoices, credit notes and receiving records on it, whatever their date.
  4. Your receiving evidence: a warehouse log, delivery documents signed by your receiver, or QuickBooks item receipts your receiver entered when goods arrived.
  5. The name or role of the person who keeps that receiving record.

Quantities QuickBooks shows as billed against a purchase order, and supplier packing slips without your receiver's mark, are not receiving evidence. Without receiving evidence of your own, the report can still check prices and duplicates, but every line reads "receipt absent". We tell you this before you pay.

Part C. The refund rule

6. What counts as found

A qualifying discrepancy is a line our rule table flags on your documents as one of: invoiced above received; invoiced above ordered; price above your tolerance; duplicate; unmatched charge; or invoiced but not received where your receiving record covers that purchase order and shows nothing. A "receipt absent" line outside that coverage also qualifies once your controller confirms in MatchDesk that the goods were not received.

7. What removes one

Only evidence: a corrected source document, a credit note, or a receiving record that meets clause 5(d). A waiver, or your controller declining to confirm, does not remove it. The same units are never counted twice.

8. What does not count either way

Lines that cannot be checked: receipt-absent lines outside your receiving record's coverage that your controller has not confirmed, possible duplicates not yet decided, and lines waiting for document review. They are listed beside the refund calculation with their count and total, so nothing is left out silently.

9. Governing report

The refund is decided once, on the report version that is current 10 business days after we deliver the first draft with no document review outstanding. That version is recorded as the governing report. Later versions do not change a refund paid or not paid.

10. The refund

If the qualifying discrepancies on the governing report total less than [CAD 500, the fee], we refund the whole fee within 10 business days, to the way you paid. Both of us can see the calculation in MatchDesk. The report keeps three figures apart: discrepancies flagged, overcharges your controller confirms, and credits actually recovered. Only the first decides the refund.

MatchDesk only reads QuickBooks and never writes to it. If you disconnect, we stop reading and using that data at once, keep your approved reports available to export for 30 days, and then delete the copied QuickBooks data. We act for you, not for Intuit.

16. Confidentiality

Your prices, suppliers and findings are your confidential information. We may say that you are a customer only with your written permission.

Part E. General

17. What the report is not

It is a rule-based exception list for your staff to review. It is not an audit, review engagement, assurance report or opinion on financial statements, and it is not legal, tax or accounting advice. You decide what to pay, withhold or claim from suppliers.

18. Liability

Our total liability under this agreement is limited to the fee you paid. Neither of us is liable for indirect loss, and we are not liable for supplier payments you make or withhold.

19. Ending early

You may withdraw permission to process your records by email at any time; processing stops and clause 15 applies. If you end the pilot before we deliver the first draft, we refund the fee in full.
