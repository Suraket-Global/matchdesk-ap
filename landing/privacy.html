Privacy notice

In short

  • We collect only what we need to run MatchDesk for your company and to answer you.
  • Your company's documents and QuickBooks data are used only to check your invoices and produce your reports.

2. Two roles: our own data, and data we process for your company

For our own business (this website, sales contact, user accounts, billing) we decide how information is used, and this notice applies directly.

For your company's records (supplier invoices, purchase orders, receiving records and QuickBooks data) we act as your company's service provider. Your company decides what is processed and stays in control of it. We use those records only to provide MatchDesk to your company, under the agreement you sign with us.

4. How we use it, and what we never do

  • We use information only for the purposes in the table above.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information or your company's records.
  • We do not compare one customer's prices, suppliers or findings with another's, even anonymised.
  • We do not use your records or QuickBooks data to train any machine-learning model.
  • We do not send marketing email without the consent Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) requires, and every message has a way to unsubscribe.

5. QuickBooks Online data

When your controller connects QuickBooks Online, MatchDesk reads purchase orders, bills, vendor credits, vendors and items from the company chosen, using Intuit's own sign-in and the "accounting" permission. MatchDesk never writes to QuickBooks. We use this data only to check your company's supplier invoices and produce its reports. We act for your company, not for Intuit.

If you disconnect, MatchDesk stops reading and stops using the QuickBooks data at once. Your approved reports stay available to export for 30 days, and the copied QuickBooks data is then deleted, or earlier on request. QuickBooks access keys are encrypted and erased at disconnect.

8. How we protect it

  • All traffic to MatchDesk uses HTTPS.
  • Each company's data is kept separate from every other company's, enforced in the database.
  • QuickBooks access keys are encrypted with keys kept apart from the database.
  • Sign-in uses one-time links; the operator account also needs a one-time code. There are no passwords to steal.
  • Our staff see a customer's records only when the customer's controller grants time-limited support access, and every visit is logged.
  • Only named people can reach the servers.

9. If something goes wrong

If we learn that your company's records were lost, accessed or disclosed without authority, we tell your company's contact the same business day with what we know, and help your company meet any duty it has to report or notify. Incidents involving QuickBooks data are reported to Intuit within 24 hours. Where we control the information and there is a real risk of significant harm, we report to the Privacy Commissioner and notify the people affected.

10. Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, how it has been used and to whom it has been disclosed, and ask us to correct it. You can withdraw consent to marketing at any time. Write to ; we answer within 30 days. If your information is held for your employer's MatchDesk account, we may refer the request to your employer, which controls it.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

11. Changes

If we change this notice, we update the date above. If a change affects how we use your company's records, we tell your controller by email before it applies.
